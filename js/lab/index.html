    WHY THE CONTRACT MATTERS

    Delivery earns payment.
    Missing delivery has a cost.

    NEAR holds the fees and bonds, enforces who can act and when, checks the answer, and records the resulting credits. Both successful delivery and a missed deadline have explicit settlement rules.

    Backup prepares the answer speculatively. This proves neither reserved physical capacity nor commercial demand. The workers are deterministic solver processes.
